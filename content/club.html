Join the GDD Community!

GDD is a great way to become a part of Cal's game development community, regardless of experience! Interested in learning? Try out our Decal! You’re also welcome to all of our semesterly events. To get involved, simply join our Discord server and you'll officially be a GDD member!

Events

GDD hosts a number of large events every semester. These include workshops, game tournaments, and Industry Speaker events, where you’ll get to hear from professionals already working in the industry!

We also have Bear Jams, our semesterly game jam. Participants have 45 hours to make a game from scratch. And, of course, we have our Semester Showcase, where you’ll get to see the incredible games the Decal teams and other members of the club have whipped up this semester!
